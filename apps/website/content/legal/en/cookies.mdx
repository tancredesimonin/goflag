## Summary

This website does not set cookies for individual tracking or targeted advertising. For that reason, no cookie consent banner is displayed. Audience measurement uses **Plausible Analytics**, which operates **without cookies** and is **hosted in the European Union**.

## Strictly necessary cookies

Only cookies or equivalent mechanisms that are **strictly necessary** for the operation of the service may be used.

### Language preference

A cookie may be stored to remember your chosen **display language** (for example French, English, Spanish, or Brazilian Portuguese), so that you do not need to select it again on each visit.

For further information, please refer to the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
